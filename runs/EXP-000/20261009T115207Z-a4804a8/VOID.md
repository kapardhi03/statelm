## What the numbers are, and are not

The run reports Cohen's κ 1.000 overall and in every category, raw agreement 1.000, value
agreement 1.000 (strict and number-aware, 19 items), and 0 disagreements across 170 items. That
is one AI session agreeing with itself. It is not inter-annotator agreement. It is not
intra-annotator agreement either, in the sense of this record's "Note on solo annotation", which
requires a human relabelling blind after at least a week.

**Not interpretable for this run:** overall κ and both of its intervals; every per-category κ,
interval and `n_either`; value agreement, strict and number-aware; the confusion matrix; both
`by_source` blocks (real and synthetic); and the `verdict` field. This holds regardless of the
10-item `n_either` floor, which four categories also failed.

**The label counts are model output as well.** `label_use`, the confusion matrix and `n_either`
describe how Codex labelled the items, not how a person would. They are not evidence about how
many abstention items the corpus contains, and they must never become gold labels (CLAUDE.md,
rule 3).
